The simple truth at the heart of her work

Athletes train their bodies. Musicians practice their scales. Almost no one is taught to train their nervous system. Yet we can all feel fabulous, once we understand the simple process and train on the inside.

Her story

She turned her pain into an instrument.

  1. 01

    The breaking point

    In college, a severe pain disorder forced Bethany to change careers. The plan she had for her life was gone, and the pain was not going away on its own.

  2. 02

    The decision

    So she did something radical. She made her own pain her biofeedback instrument and became, in her words, “intolerant of feeling miserable.”

  3. 03

    The training

    She focused her mind and watched her pain levels. Every time they dropped, she took it as a clue she was “doing something right,” and did more of it. Mind first, then body. Over and over again.

  4. 04

    The finish line

    Her pilgrimage ended with Bethany running 4 miles every day, without pain. What she learned on the way became the Consciousness Athlete body of work she teaches today.

The method

The Consciousness Athlete Lifestyle.

A method for confidently using your body as a biofeedback instrument of consciousness and grace. Bethany trains people to influence their autonomic nervous system to sustain heightened awareness, and their energy field to sustain expansion. It is very like an athlete training for a sport, or a musician practicing for a performance.

How the training works

  1. 1

    Notice

    Use your body as the instrument. Pain, tension and mood become feedback, not just problems.

  2. 2

    Shift

    Apply focused intention to calm your autonomic nervous system and change state on purpose.

  3. 3

    Sustain

    Practice like an athlete until heightened states hold longer, steadier and deeper.

  4. 4

    Live it

    Carry those states into your work, your relationships and your ordinary Tuesday.

Meet Bethany

A lifetime consciousness researcher.

Bethany Ann Gonyea, MS, has spent her life exploring one question: how do we train ourselves to feel genuinely well, and stay there?

Her understanding of intention is unusually granular, because she has had the courage to live by it. She took time away from her community-building work to document decades of study in her first book, after releasing several audio programs over the years. Her mission is massive and deliberate: to bring this empowering information to as many people as possible, across the globe.
